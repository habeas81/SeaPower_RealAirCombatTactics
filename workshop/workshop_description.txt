[h1]Real Air Combat Tactics[/h1]

Overhauls fixed-wing fighter AI in Sea Power: smarter intercepts, missile discipline, wingman tactics, tanker-aware fuel management, and a Doppler-notch radar model with automated missile defence. Works for AI and player aircraft on every side.

[b]REQUIRES: Sea Power 0.9.0+ and Anchor Chain 1.2.0[/b] (Workshop item 3380210757) with its 1.2.0 preloader. See Installation below.

[h2]Offence[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Lead-pursuit intercepts:[/b] fighters aim where the target will be, with arc-aware lead, pursuit speed management and hot pursuit.
[*][b]Shoot-look-shoot:[/b] no second missile at a target that already has one in flight.
[*][b]Crew-skill launch range:[/b] green crews fire early, near the missile's published maximum; veterans wait for a surer shot.
[*][b]Radar discipline:[/b] without a fire-and-forget missile, the radar stays off until it is needed for the shot.
[*][b]Post-launch crank[/b] and [b]pressure shots[/b].
[*][b]2-ship tactics:[/b] combat spread, target deconfliction, and a wingman extend-and-turn-in when the lead is stuck in a turning fight.
[*][b]Right-click attack orders:[/b] a selected formation intercepts with its longest-range suitable missile.
[*][b]Combat jettison:[/b] bombs, cluster stores and drop tanks are dumped when the aircraft is threatened in the air; guided air-to-ground and anti-ship missiles are kept. Set per weapon status in the config.
[/list]

[h2]Missile defence[/h2]
[list]
[*]Notch, chaff, Split-S / Immelman, hold and weave, re-notch at pitbull, last-ditch pull. Wingmen defend too.
[*]A pilot fighting for his life ignores steering orders ("Unable, defending") and follows them once the missile is defeated.
[*]After defeating a missile, or when a wingman is shot at or shot down, AI aircraft search toward the threat with radar on.
[*]A defending aircraft drops its attack and re-engages when safe.
[/list]

[h2]Radar and notching[/h2]
[list]
[*]Doppler notch model for aircraft radars and missile seekers; chaff widens the notch. Every stock airborne radar is classified as pulse-Doppler, MTI or pulse (cannot be notched).
[*]A missile's mid-course guidance drops while the shooter's radar cannot see a notched target.
[*]Player notch assist: NOTCH button on the bottom bar and in the map right-click menu.
[*]Emissions overlay shows what each unit is really radiating, using only what your side could know.
[/list]

[h2]Fuel, crews and map[/h2]
[list]
[*]Tanker-aware bingo: low-fuel fighters head for the tanker's planned route.
[*]Crew skill (Green to Ultra) sets reaction time, notch accuracy, last-ditch timing and launch range.
[*]Map status labels on aircraft (DEF-..., NOTCH, EXT, CRANK, PRESS, PUR, ATT) and missiles (GUIDED, COAST, NOTCHED, ACTIVE, IR).
[*]Fix for aircraft blocking each other while taxiing on a deck or airfield.
[/list]

[h2]Installation[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Install Anchor Chain as described on its Workshop page, including its 1.2.0 preloader.
[*]Subscribe to this mod, enable it and Anchor Chain in the [b]Mods[/b] menu, then quit to desktop and restart.
[*]Check that [i]Sea Power\BepInEx\SeaPowerFighterAI.log[/i] exists.
[/olist]
Remove any local copy of the mod so it is not loaded twice.

[h2]Status[/h2]
Beta. Known issues: occasional speed loss in sustained pursuit; some priority conflicts between hot pursuit and other behaviours; semi-active (CW) radar notching is not fully modelled.

Settings are in [i]Sea Power\BepInEx\config\com.habeas81.seapower.realaircombattactics.cfg[/i]. Please report bugs with [i]SeaPowerFighterAI.log[/i] attached (set Diagnostics = true for more detail).
