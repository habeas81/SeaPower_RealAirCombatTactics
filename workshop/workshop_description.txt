[h1]Real Air Combat Tactics (v0.9.4 Beta)[/h1]

Overhauls fixed-wing fighter AI in Sea Power: smarter intercepts, missile discipline, wingman tactics, tanker-aware fuel management, and a Doppler-notch radar model with automated missile defence. It applies to AI and player-controlled fixed-wing aircraft on every side, with a few manual tools for the player.

[b]REQUIRES: Sea Power 0.9.0+ and Anchor Chain 1.2.0[/b] (Workshop item 3380210757), installed with its [b]1.2.0 preloader[/b]. See "Installation" below.

[h2]Offence[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Shoot-look-shoot:[/b] no second missile at a target that already has a live missile in flight.
[*][b]Lead-pursuit intercepts:[/b] fighters aim where the target will be, with arc-aware lead, pursuit speed management and hot pursuit.
[*][b]Crew-skill launch range:[/b] green crews fire early, near the missile's published maximum; seasoned crews at the game's computed range; veterans wait for a closer, surer shot.
[*][b]Right-click attack orders:[/b] right-click a hostile aircraft with a formation selected and it intercepts with its longest-range suitable missile.
[*][b]Post-launch crank[/b] (radar kept on the target) and [b]pressure shots[/b].
[*][b]Radar discipline:[/b] without a Fox-3 weapon, radar stays off until kinematic range plus lock time plus 60 s.
[*][b]2-ship tactics:[/b] combat spread and target deconfliction, no redundant salvos.
[*][b]Wingman extend-and-turn-in:[/b] when the lead is stuck in a turning fight, the wingman extends and turns back in for a missile shot.
[*][b]Combat jettison:[/b] aircraft carrying bombs, cluster stores or drop tanks dump them when threatened in the air, to fight clean (guided air-to-ground and anti-ship missiles are kept). [b]Engaging strike aircraft makes them lose their bombs.[/b] The config sets which weapon statuses allow it (default Hold and Tight; set Free to keep a strike committed) and how many bombs per release.
[/list]

[h2]Fuel and tankers[/h2]
[list]
[*]Tanker-aware bingo: a fighter low on fuel heads for the tanker's planned route, not its current position.
[*]Taxi deadlock fix for aircraft blocking each other on a deck or airfield.
[/list]

[h2]Radar and notching[/h2]
[list]
[*]A passive Doppler notch model for aircraft radars and missile seekers. Flying at the right angle to the radar's beam hides a target in ground clutter. Chaff widens the notch for a short time.
[*]The mid-course guidance link is cut while the shooter's radar cannot see a notched target, and the missile coasts on its last track.
[*][b]Notch assist for the player:[/b] a NOTCH button on the bottom bar, and an entry on the map right-click menu.
[*][b]Emissions overlay:[/b] the map's emissions layer tags units with what is really radiating (search / fire-control / sonar), using only information your side could know.
[/list]

[h2]Automated missile defence[/h2]
[list]
[*]On an incoming missile: notch, chaff, Split-S / Immelman reversal, hold and weave, re-notch at pitbull, last-ditch pull. Wingmen defend the same way.
[*][b]Fighting for its life:[/b] your steering orders wait until a missile is defeated ("Unable, defending"), then are followed.
[*][b]Post-defence search:[/b] after defeating a missile, or when a wingman is shot at or shot down, AI aircraft weave toward the threat with radar on for a few minutes (DEF-SEARCH).
[*][b]Offence vs defence:[/b] a defending aircraft drops its attack (finishing a short missile support first) and re-engages when safe.
[*][b]Map status labels[/b] on friendly aircraft (DEF-..., NOTCH, EXT, CRANK, PRESS, PUR, ATT) and missiles (GUIDED, COAST, NOTCHED, ACTIVE, IR).
[/list]

[h2]Crew skill[/h2]
[list]
[*]Crew skill from Green to Ultra scales reaction delay, notch accuracy, last-ditch timing and air-to-air launch range, so better crews fight better.
[/list]

[h2]Installation[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Install Anchor Chain following its instructions, including the [b]1.2.0 preloader[/b] (ACPreloader.zip from https://github.com/SeaPower-Modders/AnchorChain/releases); older preloaders do not start it on Sea Power 0.9.0.
[*]Subscribe to Anchor Chain and this mod, enable both in the [b]Mods[/b] menu, then [b]quit to desktop and restart[/b] (mods load only at game start).
[*]Check that [i]Sea Power\BepInEx\SeaPowerFighterAI.log[/i] exists and starts with the mod's startup line.
[/olist]
If you used a local copy before, remove it so the mod is not loaded twice.
