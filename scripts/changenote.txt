[b]v0.9.4 Beta[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Updated for Sea Power 0.9.0.[/b] Requires Anchor Chain 1.2.0 with its 1.2.0 preloader; Anchor Chain (Beta Fix) is no longer needed.
[*][b]Fixed:[/b] aircraft could ignore your orders after a missile defence, notch or wingman extend, flying on at a stale point, or stay pinned at an old altitude.
[*][b]Fixed:[/b] the defensive Split-S could carry on into the climbing half of a loop.
[*][b]Fixed:[/b] speed handling during pursuit and defence on 0.9.0 (speed was re-commanded constantly and Mach not restored after a defence).
[*][b]Fixed:[/b] wingman extend could send the jet climbing toward its ceiling.
[*][b]Changed:[/b] fighters now fire Sparrow-class missiles at the game's own range instead of chasing for a Sidewinder shot. Sea Power 0.9.0 already accounts for target speed and evasion, so the mod's extra range reduction is removed. Crew skill sets the launch range instead: green crews fire early, near the missile's published maximum (easy to defeat); veterans hold for a closer, surer shot.
[*][b]New:[/b] a pilot fighting for his life does not obey steering orders. An order given during a missile defence shows "Unable, defending" and is followed once the threat is defeated. Orders with no threat to life are obeyed at once.
[*][b]New:[/b] post-defence search. After surviving a missile, an AI aircraft with nothing to re-engage weaves toward where the shot came from for a few minutes, radar on, checking its six, instead of flying straight on. Wingmen of an aircraft that is shot at or shot down do the same. Map tag DEF-SEARCH.
[*][b]New:[/b] every stock airborne radar is classified for the notch model from open sources. The F-4J's AWG-10 and the F-14's AWG-9 are early pulse-Doppler; MiG-23ML/MiG-25PD and Mirage F1 radars get a wider, cruder notch; Blue Fox and the F-4D/E and F-5E radars cannot be notched.
[*][b]Fixed:[/b] the NOTCH bottom-bar button tooltip showed "MISSING TEXT".
[*][b]Removed:[/b] the vertical-arc fix; Sea Power 0.9.0 fixed that game bug itself.
[/list]
